🏥 MedClear AI – Multi-Agent Healthcare Swarm

Autonomous Prescription Simplifier & Caregiver Routine Planner using CrewAI and Groq

MedClear AI is an intelligent Multi-Agent Healthcare Assistant designed to simplify complex medical prescriptions, decode medical abbreviations, identify medication safety precautions, and generate personalized medication schedules.

Powered by CrewAI, Groq API, and GPT-OSS 120B, the system uses four specialized AI agents working collaboratively to transform confusing medical prescriptions into easy-to-understand patient care plans.

MedClear AI also supports multilingual communication, allowing patients and caregivers to receive explanations in their preferred language, including English, Urdu, Roman Urdu, Arabic, Hindi, Spanish, French, and other languages.

🚀 Key Features

* Multi-Agent Architecture: Four specialized AI agents collaborate through a sequential workflow.

* Prescription Decryption: Converts complicated medical prescriptions into simple explanations.

* Medical Abbreviation Decoder: Explains OD, BD, TDS, AC, PC, HS, SOS, PRN, and other medical abbreviations.

* Drug Safety Analysis: Identifies medication precautions, food interactions, and important safety instructions.

* Personalized Medication Scheduling: Creates a chronological 24-hour medication schedule based on patient routines.

7-Day Medication Tracker: Generates a structured medication adherence checklist.

* Emergency Red-Flag Detection: Highlights symptoms that may require medical attention.

* Doctor Consultation Preparation: Generates important questions for patients and caregivers to discuss with their doctors.

* Multilingual AI Responses: Detects the user's language and generates patient-facing explanations in the same language.

* Groq-Powered LLM: Uses Groq's OpenAI-compatible API with GPT-OSS 120B.

🤖 Multi-Agent Architecture

MedClear AI consists of four specialized agents operating through a sequential CrewAI workflow.

1. Clinical Pharmacist & Prescription Decrypter

Responsibilities:

* Analyze doctor's prescriptions.

* Decode medical abbreviations and Latin shorthand.

* Identify medicine names and strengths.

* Explain what each medicine is used for.

* Simplify dosage and administration instructions.

2. Pharmacovigilance & Drug Safety Auditor

Responsibilities:

* Analyze medication safety precautions.

* Identify food and drug interactions.

* Highlight grapefruit and dietary restrictions where applicable.

* Explain important medication timing instructions.

* Provide medication-related Dos and Don'ts.

3. Caregiver Routine Architect

Responsibilities:

* Analyze the patient's daily routine.

* Create a personalized 24-hour medication schedule.

* Organize medicines around breakfast, lunch, dinner, and bedtime.

* Generate a medication checklist.

* Help caregivers track medication adherence.

4. Patient Advocate & Emergency Clinical Navigator

Responsibilities:

* Identify important medical warning signs.

* Prepare emergency red-flag guidance.

* Explain when patients should contact their doctors.

* Generate four useful questions for the next medical consultation.

* Prepare the final patient care plan.

⚠️ Medical Disclaimer

MedClear AI is an experimental healthcare assistance and prescription simplification project.

It is designed to support patients and caregivers in understanding prescriptions and organizing medication routines.

It does not replace professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

All medication schedules, dosage instructions, drug interactions, and emergency recommendations must be verified by a qualified healthcare professional or pharmacist before clinical use.

Never start, stop, or change prescribed medication based solely on AI-generated recommendations.
